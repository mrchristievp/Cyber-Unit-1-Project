AP CYBERSECURITY • UNIT 1 INVESTIGATION

Case File 01: The Friday Afternoon Incident

A school security team has received several suspicious reports. Determine what the evidence supports — and what still needs verification.

CASE BRIEFING

Your Assignment

Junior Security Analyst

What happened?

At approximately 2:30 PM on Friday, school staff and students began reporting suspicious messages and unusual account activity. The technology team does not yet know whether all of the reports are connected.

Your team has been given the available incident evidence. Analyze it carefully before deciding what you think occurred.

Questions your team must answer

  • What facts are directly supported by the evidence?
  • What conclusions are likely, but not yet proven?
  • Which events may be unrelated?
  • What should the school do immediately?
  • What should investigators verify next?

Analyst rule

Do not force every clue into one story. Some artifacts may support the same attack chain. Others may be unrelated, ambiguous, or incomplete.

INVESTIGATION GUIDE

Complete Your Team Worksheet

Work in groups of 2 or 3. Discuss each part, agree on your reasoning, and record your answers on the worksheet. Cite specific artifact numbers.

1. What Happened?

Choose the 5 most important artifacts. Record what each shows and what it might mean. Connect two artifacts and explain their relationship.

2. Known, Likely, or Possible?

Use Known, Likely, Possible, or Not Enough Evidence to classify the worksheet's six claims about the fake login page, account compromise, coffee shop Wi-Fi, AI-generated payroll email, follow-up targeting, and parent voicemail. Support each classification with evidence.

Choose one claim that cannot be proven yet. What additional evidence would you want?

3. Reconstruct the Attack

Complete the most likely chain: suspicious message → victim action → attacker gains → authentication activity → account access → follow-up activity.

Cite supporting artifacts for each step. Where could the attack most easily have been stopped, and why?

4. Response

Assume your team is called as soon as the suspicious login succeeds. Choose the 3 most important immediate actions and 2 longer-term improvements for the school. Explain which risk each immediate action addresses and why you recommend each improvement.

5. AI + Human Judgment

Review Artifact 10. Identify one conclusion the AI supports well and one where it is too confident. Explain your reasoning with evidence.

Why should a human analyst still review the AI's conclusions?

6. Final Incident Report

Each student writes their own 4–6 sentence report for the school's technology team. Explain what most likely happened, cite at least two artifact numbers, identify what remains uncertain or might be unrelated, and recommend what the school should do first and why.

Distinguish what you know from what you think is likely. Submit your individual report on MyViewPoint as a PDF.

Before you finish: Check that your conclusions cite evidence and that every group member can explain your reasoning.
